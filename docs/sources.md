## How to read the summaries

- **Required / minimum:** explicitly labeled requirements in the employer text.
- **Preferred / valued:** bonuses, preferences or employer values; not silently promoted to minimums.
- **Candidate profile:** preserves Anthropic’s softer “fit” framing.
- **Overview and portfolio guidance:** our synthesis, clearly distinguished from employer claims.
- **Status:** accessible posting/application route at the research date, not a guarantee of unfilled headcount.

No aggregators or social posts were used as evidence for requirements. Summaries paraphrase rather than reproduce descriptions. Dates refer to review time unless explicitly identified as an application deadline. No applications were submitted.

## Limits and update process

This purposive sample is not representative enough to estimate market demand, skill frequency or compensation. Two Google internships share a role family; they are not independent evidence of an industry-wide trend. Experienced roles are not substitutes for new-graduate opportunities.

Before refreshing: open each source, check title/location and application availability, re-read qualifications and logistics, update the review date, and mark removed postings as closed/unavailable instead of silently retaining an “open” label. Keep contradictions visible until the employer resolves them.
